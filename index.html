THE ARTIST

AB MUSIC_
FROM LAGOS.

AB MUSIC_ is the professional music identity of Abraham Onyebuchi, an independent artist, producer and record label founder based in Lagos, Nigeria.

The project blends Trap, Future Bass, Dark Hip-Hop, Rap and Drill with Afro-inspired sounds.

His music explores resilience, raw street storytelling, personal pain, ambition and the experiences that shape real life.

AB MUSIC_ represents both the solo artist and the wider creative ecosystem operating through AB MUSIC RECORDS LTD and AB DRILL RECORDS.

THE CORE PROJECT

BIG
SCAN ME

BIG SCAN ME is the core umbrella project and broader ecosystem created by Abraham Onyebuchi (AB) through AB MUSIC RECORDS LTD and AB DRILL RECORDS.

It goes beyond a single song. BIG SCAN ME connects music, technology, fan engagement, exclusive content and opportunities for independent artists.

01 — MUSIC PLATFORM A developing global music platform designed to connect independent artists directly with fans.
02 — MUSIC PROJECTS Music, albums, drill tracks and motivational podcast content built around the BIG SCAN ME concept.
03 — THE 1 BILLION BOOKLET An interactive ecosystem concept featuring exclusive drops, VIP experiences, hidden content and opportunities.
04 — GLOBAL VISION Bridging music distribution, technology and direct fan engagement on a global scale.
AB DRILL RECORDS ARTIST

RICH
KEL

Rich Kel is a recording artist connected to AB DRILL RECORDS whose music draws heavily from life experience and true-life stories.

His mission is to touch lives through music, spread good vibes and positive intentions, and make a difference through his lyrics.

Rich Kel represents the belief that real experiences can become music that gives people strength, happiness and something to smile about.

He continues to build a strong relationship with his fans and supporters while using his music to tell stories and inspire change.
